Holly Reinhardt

Holly Reinhardt is a young aspiring bass player currently based in London, studying at the Royal Academy of Music. She initially made herself known through the support of Tomorrows Warriors, performing for them as part of Frontline from 2024-2025, and playing alongside other exciting talent on the London scene like Emily Tran and Laurie Rich. Her new sextet, as she steps up to the position as a band leader for the first time, is influenced by a multitude of genres and styles.

Brian Blade and the Fellowship, Gerald Clayton, Trio Grande, Wayne Shorter and Classical influences like Bach and Messian are just a few of the key inspirations that contribute to her sound. The lineup consists of Ciarra Oswagu on flute, Alexander Sibley on alto saxophone, Anchit Sengputa on guitar, Angelica Coupland on piano, and Finlay McKenzie on drums.

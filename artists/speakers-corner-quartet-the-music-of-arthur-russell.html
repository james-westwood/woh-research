Speakers Corner Quartet: The Music of Arthur Russell

All 150 names Back to Soul / Jazz / Afrobeat

Speakers Corner Quartet is a band that stands testament to friendship, love and the power of music. What started as a house band in 2006 for legendary open mic night in Brixton Jamm is now a four-piece powerhouse consisting of flautist Biscuit, drummer and percussionist Kwake Bass, violinist Raven Bush, and bassist Peter Bennie. The band’s improvisational style and innovative approach to composition have defined them as a multifaceted band.

Allowing them to play music with unfeigned freedom and honesty, ever-evolving but with a sound that is distinctively their own. Making music together for more than two decades, they have been the bedrock of the musical community, passing on the baton and continuing the legacies of previous generations of South London greats. Their support for artists and influence is far-reaching; collaborating with the likes of Sampha, Kae Tempest, Tirzah and Shabaka Hutchings and developing the next wave of young talent such as Joe Armon-Jones and Léa Sen.

In October of 2021, the band made a return to the stage after 10 years in remarkable fashion as they sold out the Barbican to an audience who had yet to hear a single note. The 3 hour show presented all new music, alongside a cast of friends, family and close collaborators. Their long-awaited 2023 debut album ‘Further Out Than The Edge’ gained critical acclaim, being awarded 5 stars and one of 2023’s albums of the year by The Guardian and went on to perform iconic sets at Glastonbury, Roundhouse and Montreux Jazz Festival.

2024 has seen the band win an Ivor Novello Award for their single ‘Geronimo Blues’ featuring Kae Tempest, perform a sold-out Barbican show paying tribute to legendary Arthur Russell and playing all new compositions with a 35-piece orchestra for Chaka Khan’s Meltdown Festival. The band is currently producing a much anticipated 2nd album, pushing the orchestral and synth-focused compositions that debuted at Meltdown Festival to new levels.

In addition to this, they have also written an original score for the critically acclaimed BBC series ‘Mr Loverman’, of with the first two episodes premiering at Tribeca Film Festival 2024.

UK electronic music project Nightmares On Wax , led by producer and DJ George Evelyn, has been a consistent and evolving presence in underground music since the early 1990s. Emerging from Leeds during the rise of UK rave culture, the project quickly developed a sound that moved beyond club functionality, blending hip hop, soul, dub, funk and electronic textures into something reflective, warm and deeply human.

After early releases on Warp Records, Nightmares On Wax became known for albums that favoured mood, groove and atmosphere over rigid genre boundaries. Records such as Smoker’s Delight and Carboot Soul helped establish a template for downtempo and leftfield electronic music that centred feeling, musicality and everyday experience, resonating far beyond the club. At the core of the project is a strong sense of rhythm and space, drawing equally from soundsystem culture, sample based hip hop and live instrumentation.

Over the years, Nightmares On Wax has collaborated with a wide range of vocalists and musicians, allowing the sound to remain fluid while retaining a clear identity rooted in groove and intention. As a DJ and live performer, George Evelyn brings this same sensibility to the dancefloor, moving between downtempo, house, disco and soulful electronic music with a focus on flow and connection.

Whether in clubs, festivals or listening spaces, his sets reflect a lifetime of musical exploration and an instinctive understanding of how sound shapes shared experience. Through decades of releases, performances and quiet influence, Nightmares On Wax continues to operate as a bridge between scenes, eras and communities, offering music that invites people to slow down, tune in and move together.

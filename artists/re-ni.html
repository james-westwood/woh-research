re:ni is the alias of Lauren Reni Bush, a DJ & producer based in South East London. Born in Birmingham and later raised in Dorset, re:ni’s sub-heavy sound marries deep, dank dub sonics with dark & twisted mutations of techno, jungle and dubstep, characteristic of her label, party and radio show “re:lax” which she runs with Hessle Audio affiliate Laksa. Following her steady rise as an internationally recognised DJ, having played institutions like De School, fabric and Panorama Bar multiple times, re:ni released her debut 12″ Revenge Body on Ilian Tape in 2022.

This year saw the release of her second EP BeautySick which came out via Batu’s Timedance label, as well as her first 12″ on her own imprint.

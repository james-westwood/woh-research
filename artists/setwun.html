Setwun is the musical brainchild of singer-songwriter, composer, producer, multi-instrumentalist, and DJ – Josh Panakera-Molony. Sitting at the cross-section of many music worlds – shapeshifting through sound, instinctively soulful, his brand of futurist fusion weaves through the purview of contemporary jazz, beats and electronica with feeling. Whether psychedelic neo-soul, broken grooves, or rhythmic experimentation, Setwun makes affecting music, no matter the twist.

The Eora (Sydney) based Solomon Islander-Australian is as versatile as he is prolific. A much respected figure within Australia’s ever-fertile music scene, the hype has been bubbling with every small offering and tip-offs from some of the world’s most revered. His debut EP Our World via Mandarin Dreams and a collaboration project, Yeah Definitely with producer Hugh B, both were met with a rapid spread of support and instantly showcased his versatility of styles as a soul producer and vocalist.

A series of self released club cuts along with his contributions to CoOp Presents’ They’re Energised and Rhythm Section’s Inner West Wind Up further demonstrated his acumen for the club space and gained more overseas attention. Flexing his versatility, he added his production touch to Lex Amor’s track Summer Rain while is a constant collaborator with some of Australia’s finest, such as Silentjay, Kuzich, Horatio Luna, Pa777ience, INQ., Nikodimos, Glo and many more.

In 2025 he launched Soulstravision with fellow selector Alex Intas – an incubator for their community set to showcase many of Australia’s next up. Setwun has been invited to support a diverse list of revered artists from Amp Fiddler, Kokoroko, Nubya Garcia, Thee Sacred Souls, Dennis Bovell, John Carroll Kirby, Maverick Sabre, Digable Planets, DJ Shadow, and Chaka Khan. As a club DJ, he remains one of Australia’s most in-demand.

He’s guested for the likes of cult party purveyors Brighter Days and Touching Bass and broadcast transmissions through NTS, Worldwide FM & Rinse FM. His latest EP Visions in Venus out now via London label Extra Soul Perception has further turned heads far and wide. A four-track body of psyche-soul rooted expression and rhythmic finesse that blends groove, emotion and instrumental mastery in equal measure

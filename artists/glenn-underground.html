A true custodian of Chicago house culture, Glenn Underground is a producer and DJ whose sound is rooted in groove, soul and musical freedom. Emerging from Chicago’s rich lineage of house, jazz and gospel, Glenn has spent decades shaping a sound that feels both deeply classic and endlessly forward moving. His productions are warm, raw and human led, built around rolling basslines, live feeling keys and rhythms that invite dancers into a shared moment rather than a fixed destination.

Whether behind the decks or in the studio, Glenn approaches music as a living conversation. His DJ sets unfold patiently, drawing from deep house, jazz funk and disco, creating spaces where dancers connect not just to the beat, but to each other. A longtime torchbearer for soulful dance music, Glenn Underground continues to remind us why Chicago house remains one of the most vital musical languages in the world.

Joy Crookes is a British singer-songwriter from South London known for her rich, soulful voice and sharp, socially aware songwriting. Blending soul, jazz, R&B, and pop, her music often explores identity, politics, and love with wit and emotional depth. She broke through with a run of acclaimed EPs before releasing her debut album Skin , which cemented her as one of the UK’s most distinctive new voices.

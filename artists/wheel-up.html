Danny Wheeler, who styles the name WheelUP, is from West London and is of Zimbabwean and British descent. He spent years in jungle and drum & bass under his own name, running the W10 Records label, before turning to the broken beat scene that grew up in the same part of the city. Afronaut, who founded Bugz in the Attic and helped invent the sound, gave him the WheelUP name, and Bugz in the Attic later made him an honorary member. The lineage he plays in runs back through them to 4hero.

Bruk is broken beat: syncopated, off-grid drums at house tempo, with jazz and boogie harmony over the top. His first record under the name was 'Self Healing Machine' for Tru Thoughts, and the albums 'Good Love' (2021), 'We Are The Magic' (2023) and 'Inner Light' followed on the same label, pulling in hip-hop, neo-soul and nu-jazz and featuring Afronaut, Kaidi Tatham, Abacus and Tiawa among others.

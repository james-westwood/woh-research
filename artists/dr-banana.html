London based DJ, producer and label head Dr Banana has become a key figure in the city’s contemporary club landscape. Rooted in UK garage, house and bass culture, his work reflects a deep engagement with dancefloor history alongside a forward facing approach to production and curation. As a producer, Dr Banana’s output draws heavily on the swing, weight and warmth of classic UKG, filtering those influences through modern club sensibilities.

His tracks and remixes are characterised by playful rhythms, low end focus and an understanding of how records function in the room, shaped as much by DJ experience as studio practice. Beyond his own releases, Dr Banana is the founder of the Dr Banana label, which has become a platform for new and emerging voices within UK garage and adjacent sounds. The label’s catalogue centres dancefloor ready music while maintaining a strong sense of identity, community and continuity with the genre’s roots.

As a DJ, Dr Banana is known for energetic, groove led sets that move fluidly between garage, house, breaks and bass driven music. He has become a regular presence across clubs and festivals in the UK and internationally, contributing to a broader resurgence and recontextualisation of UK garage within contemporary club culture. Through DJing, production and label work, Dr Banana continues to operate at the intersection of past and present, supporting new talent while keeping the communal, physical spirit of UK dance music at the centre of his work.

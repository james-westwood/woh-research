Actress is an artist and producer based in London, with an expansive discography of over ten LP’s. Across multiple releases for Ninja Tune and via his own Werkdiscs imprint, he’s created his own arcane language filled with hermetic references and inter-dimensional electronic composition. Most recently Actress aligned with Norwegian label Smalltown Supersound for the Statik LP and released the Tranzkript 1 EP on Modern Obscure Music.

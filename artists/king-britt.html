Philadelphia-born Pew Fellow King James Britt (his given name) is a producer, composer, performer, and Full Teaching Professor of Computer Music at the University of California San Diego, where he integrates three decades of professional practice into a curriculum focused on contemporary electronic music, creative research, and culturally engaged pedagogy. His work emphasizes non linear approaches to technology, improvisation, and Black musical innovation, shaping new models of electronic music education.

As a composer and producer, he has collaborated with De La Soul, Madlib, Kathy Sledge, and directors Michael Mann & Khalil Joseph , and has been commissioned to remix artists including Miles Davis, James Brown, Solange, and Calvin Harris | Dua Lipa. He recently released a studio project with MacArthur Fellow and Pulitzer Prize winner, Tyshawn Sorey. A globally active performer, he has appeared at AfroPunk, Berghain, MoogFest, and Le Guess Who, and was the original DJ for the Grammy-winning Digable Planets.

He also created the groundbreaking course & festival, Blacktronika, Afrofuturism in Electronic Music that honors the innovators of color in electronic music that have been overlooked within the historical narrative. Guests in the class have included Herbie Hancock, Questlove, Patrice Rushen and many more. Expanding Charlie Dark’s original Blacktronika forward.

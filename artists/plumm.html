Plumm

All 143 names Back to Soul / Jazz / Afrobeat

Plumm is a South London-based vocalist, composer, multi-instrumentalist, and bandleader with a Welsh core, creating a sound that sits at the intersection of trip-hop, alternative electronica, and the UK’s evolving jazz underground. Her self-released track You Are The One (Live) features as the title track for the Apple TV series Liaison , marking a significant crossover into screen and international audiences.

Plumm’s music is dark, ethereal, and vocal-led—blending improvisation with electronic textures and anthemic songwriting. Drawing inspiration from Cocteau Twins, Massive Attack and Björk. Catch her as Jamz Supernova’s ‘After Dark Discovery’ and Clash Magazines “one-to-watch” Alongside her solo work, Plumm is a prolific collaborator. She performs and creates across a wide spectrum of projects including Levitation Orchestra, Marla Kether, NYX, Marysia Osu, Plumm presents Led Zeppelin , trance-house duo Plumm x Hen.D, and BigSabbathMode; demonstrating her versatility and deep roots in London’s experimental music scene. Her vocal sample pack GarageVol.1 is also out now, expanding her creative output into production and sound design.

Dave Okumu presents DVTN: A Prayer For D’Angelo A tribute to the late neo soul legend, performed by the revered Austro-British musician and his community of collaborators. “Following the recent news of his death, many of us have felt a need to come together in this unique moment of grief and gratitude. Through his performance and recordings, he has been a companion to our community, inspiring, shaping and influencing approaches and intentions in subtle and far reaching ways. We are devastated and grateful in equal measure. We hope you can join us for an performance steeped in his music.”

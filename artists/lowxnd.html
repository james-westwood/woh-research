Compelled by his profound love of music, Nathan Dawkins/LowxND is a London-based bassist swiftly making a name for himself amongst some of London’s finest musicians. He has performed in renowned venues such as Ronnie Scott’s Jazz Club andJazz Cafe. Also playing festivals like St.Lucia Jazz festival, Cross the Tracks and Bologna Jazz festival. Respecting the past and pushing the music of the present, his music blends Jazz, R’n’B and Hip-hop whilst paying homage to his Black-British roots.

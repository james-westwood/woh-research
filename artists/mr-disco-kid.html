Mr. Disco Kid began his career as a DJ and private record dealer in his teens. With his extensive knowledge of vinyl records, he has mastered the ability to curate a wide range of sounds, from unknown and rare grooves to house, techno, and world music, all while maintaining his unique groove and a musical vision that transcends genres. In 2024 and 2025 he will embark on a DJ tour across Europe.

He performed a remarkable 7-hour set at Giant Steps in London, earning recognition from various corners of the music world. In both 2024 and 2025, he successfully delivered all-night-long sets at Giant Steps in London. As a record dealer, he counts many renowned DJs and collectors, such as Gilles Peterson, Tuxedo, and Antal, among his clients. His passion lies in unearthing undiscovered records, creating value for them, and sharing them with the world.

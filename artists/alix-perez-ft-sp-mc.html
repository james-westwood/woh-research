Alix Perez Belgium born, London based DJ and producer Alix Perez has become a central figure in contemporary bass music through a practice rooted in depth, precision and long term exploration. Known for moving fluidly between drum and bass, dubstep, jungle and half time sounds, his work reflects a deep understanding of soundsystem culture and club dynamics. Emerging in the mid 2000s, Alix built his reputation through a steady run of productions and DJ sets that prioritised low end weight, groove and atmosphere.

Rather than following trends, his output has consistently focused on balance and restraint, allowing space and tension to shape both his records and performances. As a DJ, Alix Perez is recognised for carefully constructed sets that reward patience and attention. His selections draw from across the bass spectrum, blending classics, dubplates and new material into journeys that feel considered and grounded in the room.

Whether playing late night sessions or large scale festival stages, his approach remains focused on sound, system and flow. Beyond his solo work, Alix has played a key role in building platforms for collaboration and community, most notably through projects such as 1985 Music, which has supported a wide range of artists working across drum and bass and experimental bass music. This curatorial approach mirrors his broader ethos, centred on longevity, trust and shared growth.

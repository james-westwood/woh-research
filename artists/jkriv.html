JKriv is a Brooklyn-based producer, DJ, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and labelowner. Widely known as a co-founder of labelRazor-N-Tape, J is also a highlyrespected producer, prolific remixer, and DJ. In a music career stretching 2 decades, JKriv has blazed an unconventional paththrough club world, beginning as a trained jazz musician and live performer beforehoning his studio and DJ skills.

JKriv’s prolific production work has yielded scoresof releases onRazor-N-Tape,SoundwayRecords ,Heist Recordings,MotonRecords,ZRecords, and many other labels. As a songwriter, arranger, collaborator, and studio musician, J has worked with luminary artistsDimitriFromParis,HorseMeatDisco feat. KathySledge,AdiOasis,JohnMorales,DamesBrownand many more. JKriv’s music has garnered support from all quarters ofclub and radio DJs, such as Gilles Peterson, Antal, Hunee,Colleen“Cosmo”Murphy,DerrickCarter,Disclosure,AndrewWeatherall,LouieVega,KennyDope,CraigCharles,andJamzSupernova, to name a few.J dropped loads of music in 2025, including his Intuition EPon Razor-N-Tape, theAdaptationEPon Rocksteady Disco, an exclusive song on Quantic’sK7D JKicks compilation, and remixes onUbiquity,WahWah45s,LumberjacksinHell,JulietRecords.

JKriv’s 2026 release schedule includes collaborations with keyboardist Jason Lindner and Peter Matson respectively on Razor-N-Tape and Favorite Recordings, a track on aMake-A-Dance12” comp, numerous remixes nd a full length live album fromAJoyfulNoise, a project produced musical directed by J documenting 3 years of events at Brooklyn clubPublicRecords.As co-founder of Brooklyn labelRazor-N-Tape, JKriv has had a sizable impact on both the local NY and global dance music scenes.

The label’s prolific andconsistent quality output across 150+releases over 12 years of operation hasearned them eminent respect and support from club DJs, radio selectors andlisteners around the world. The Razor-N-Tape Brooklyn shop, now in its 4th year ofoperation, is an established community hub for the local dance music scene.A resident onTheLotRadio, JKriv’s DJ sets showcase his deep and diverse taste,weaving eclectic disco, house, world grooves, and offbeat selections togetherseamlessly.

J has also recently been a featured guests onNTSRadio,OpenlabIbiza,BalamiiRadioand hosted his own regular “Living Sounds” show on RadioAlhara throughout lockdown.With a busy stateside touring schedule in early 2026 including Dante’sHifi( Miami),MadRadio( NYC & Miami),PublicRecords, Mustache Bash(San Diego),Groovewell Vacation(California), this summer JKriv will be returning to GillesPeterson’sWeOutHerefestival for the second time along with numerous otherUK/EU dates.

London based DJ, broadcaster and cultural tastemaker Alexander Nut has long been a vital thread in the fabric of the city’s underground music culture. Best known as a founding resident of NTS Radio, his journey is rooted in deep listening, curiosity and an unwavering commitment to sharing music that exists beyond trends or easy categorisation. Raised on a wide spectrum of sounds, Alexander’s musical world spans jazz, soul, dub, hip hop, ambient, experimental electronics and leftfield club music.

His approach is guided by atmosphere and narrative rather than genre, with selections that invite listeners to slow down, tune in and discover unexpected connections across eras and styles. Through his long running NTS radio shows, Alexander has played a key role in shaping the station’s identity as a home for adventurous, open minded music. His broadcasts are intimate and exploratory, often spotlighting overlooked records, deep cuts and new artists alongside timeless classics, all presented with a calm authority and deep respect for the music.

As a DJ, Alexander Nut brings the same sensibility to clubs, festivals and listening spaces. His sets are patient, immersive and finely balanced, creating environments that reward attention and trust. Whether playing late night rooms or sunlit festival stages, his focus remains on building mood, space and emotional resonance. Respected for his taste, consistency and quiet influence, Alexander Nut continues to operate as a connector within the music community.

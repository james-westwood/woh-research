Finn Hori-Ohrstrom is an exciting new trumpet voice on London’s jazz scene – offering a soulful, fresh and joyous take on a mix of standards and modern classics. Currently studying jazz at Trinity Laban Conservatoire, Finn has come up through Tomorrow’s Warriors, mentored by Binker Golding, Gerard Presencer and Axel Kaner-Lidström. Already making his mark on the scene, Finn has performed at iconic venues such as The Jazz Cafe, The Vortex, Queen Elizabeth Hall and Dizzy’s Club at New York’s Lincoln Centre.

Inspired by the soulful sounds of Roy Hargrove and Sean Mason, the fearless creativity of Miles Davis and Herbie Hancock, this performance will centre around fresh, inventive interpretations of songbook standards and modern classics.

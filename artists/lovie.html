Lovie

Lovie’s musical discovery began in the digital music age: a young lover of local radio and after-school MTV marathons, her early mixes took shape in the form of burned CDs she made for her friends. After relocating from her hometown of Washington, D.C. to New York in 2020, Lovie’s unique selection of soulful, spiritual music swiftly ushered her into New York’s local music scene.

At the taste-making Brooklyn radio station, Lot Radio, she gave birth to her homey platform, summer school radio. For four years Lovie’s radio mixes—now being offered as one of the station’s few weekly residencies—have thumbed through the intimate corners of her music collection. She saunters through slow-groove disco, dusty ’70s funk and groovy ’80s jazz at a studied pace that makes it easy to marvel at every timeless chime, trill, and drum beat.

She’s as tempted by the bedroom pop of Clairo as much as the cosmic funk of Lonnie Liston Smith. Outside the Lot, Lovie presents summer school radio as soothing listening sessions around Brooklyn, providing warm balms for city fatigue with artists like alt-jazz harpist muva of Earth and New Age legend Laraaji. As co-founder of Soul Connection with long-time friend and collaborator, Honey Bun, she imbues the power of friendship and Black femme empowerment into sumptuous house music and ’90s R&B spun with the patience and musicality associated with the East Coast house tradition.

Connecting the mind, to the body, to the soul, her club sets inspire the warmest, most community-minded dance floors you’ll find in the city. Her diligence to the craft, musical curiosity, and deep focus on the listening experience has earned her a spot on Mixmag’s Top 25 Breakthrough DJs of 2024. She now holds a coveted residency at Brooklyn club public records, and has played at art centers like MoMA, Brooklyn Academy of Music and The Beacon Theater, where she opened for Kamasi Washington.

Her work has also brought her to international gigs at Paris’s Djoon, London’s Spiritland, and more. Whether playing on her stomping grounds or rocking dance floors overseas, Lovie creates honey-sweet dance floor memories you’ll want to replay again and again.

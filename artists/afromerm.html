afromerm is the Oram and YMA Music Producer Award-winning project of Cecilia Morgan, a South London-based composer and sound artist blending electronic/organic textures, voice, live instrumentation, and her self-built motion-reactive instrument, Juniper. Featured on BBC Radio 1 as a ‘sonic innovator’, with performance credits spanning from Barbican Hall and London Jazz Festival to Paradiso Amsterdam, afromerm immerses the listener in the elemental mythology of her soundworld.

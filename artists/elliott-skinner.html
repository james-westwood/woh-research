Elliott Skinner is a Texas-raised, Brooklyn-based artist with a sound that blurs the lines between folk, soul, rock, and blues. A magnetic performer and multi-instrumentalist, his music reflects the roots and textures of Black culture in his music. Skinner has opened for Saya Gray, Hiatus Kaiyote, Amos Lee, and Kamasi Washington, and served as music director and bassist for Leon Bridges’ touring band.

With a new album on the way in 2026, he’s set to bring his powerful voice, improvisational spirit, and energy to stages all over the world.

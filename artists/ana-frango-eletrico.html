Ana Frango Elétrico is a singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, producer, and visual artist. Hailing from Rio de Janeiro’s vibrant independent scene, award-winning she is known for her genre-bending sound that defies easy labels. Her music expertly fuses Brazilian traditions—like bossa nova, MPB, samba, and Tropicália—with a modern mix of indie pop, funk, post-punk, and jazz.

The result is a style that is both playful and sophisticated. Her spectacular concerts are wild and sensitive, showcasing music at the highest level. This eclectic approach has captivated critics and cemented her reputation as a true innovator in modern Brazilian music.

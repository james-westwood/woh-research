AliA

AliA combines a fierce and passionate love for a variety of genres with a remarkable ability to seamlessly blend between them. Each set is an uncompromising genre -hopping story guaranteed to keep you on your toes which lets AliA play on lineups with techno heavyweights lian Tape, to dubstep stalwarts HVYWGHT, and One House’s very own Mr Scruff. Being only in her early 20s, the sky’s the limit for this talented young DJ.

‘Eclectic’ is thrown around quite casually these days, but AliA is a pretty solid demonstration of the word. With a bag of tunes and tricks as deep as they come, whether it’s through soulful jazz explorations or letting huge low ends loose AliA is always primed to make a splash and get any crowd going.

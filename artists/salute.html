Salute

Vienna by way of Manchester DJ and producer salute has emerged as one of the most distinctive voices in contemporary club music, blending emotional songwriting with a deep understanding of UK dancefloor culture. Drawing from garage, grime, dubstep and house, their sound balances warmth, momentum and melody, shaped by years spent immersed in clubs, radio and community led spaces.

salute was first initiated into the UK club scene at 18 after moving to Brighton and later Manchester, where early exposure to club culture helped refine their approach as both a DJ and producer. What began as an exploration inspired by online sets and Boiler Room videos quickly evolved into a hands on understanding of sound system energy, crowd dynamics and genre fluidity. In 2024, salute announced their forthcoming album TRUE MAGIC , due July 12th on Ninja Tune.

The record marks a shift toward a more collaborative process, developed through shared studio time with peers and featuring contributions from artists including Disclosure, Rina Sawayama, Empress Of, Karma Kid, Sam Gellaitry, piri, Léa Sen, LEILAH and Nakamura Minami. Lead single “saving flowers”, a collaboration with Rina Sawayama, set the tone for the project, pairing summer ready energy with a strong sense of narrative and motion.

Inspired aesthetically and sonically by old Japanese car adverts and VHS era visuals, TRUE MAGIC centres around the idea of momentum and movement, with salute taking a pop informed approach to songwriting while maintaining a clear connection to club music’s physical roots. The album reflects a belief in collaboration as a creative tool, prioritising shared process and organic development over isolation.

As a DJ, salute is known for expressive, high energy sets that move fluidly between genres while maintaining a strong emotional throughline. Their live catalogue includes performances at Coachella’s Do Lab, Boiler Room Melbourne, multiple appearances at Glastonbury, a BBC Radio 1 Essential Mix live with Annie Mac and The Blessed Madonna, and international shows across Europe, Asia and the US.

Recent performances include b2b sets with Yung Singh, DJ Boring and Pretty Girl, as well as appearances alongside Disclosure, Mall Grab, Hudson Mohawke and Jungle. With a 2024 MOBO Award nomination for Best Electronic or Dance Act and a growing global presence, salute continues to build a body of work rooted in connection, collaboration and the shared experience of the dancefloor.

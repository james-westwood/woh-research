London based DJ, broadcaster and tastemaker Dom Servini is a stalwart of the city’s eclectic music scene. As co owner of the Wah Wah 45s record label and an internationally respected turntablist, Dom not only knows how to move a dancefloor but has also played a key role in championing and releasing artists including Dele Sosimi, Soothsayers, Kotoa, Lawne and many more. Beyond the label, Dom is an experienced radio and club DJ as well as a music journalist.

He can be heard weekly on his Jazztronics show on Jazz FM, alongside monthly broadcasts on Soho Radio and One Jazz, where his deep knowledge and open minded approach to sound are on full display. On the dancefloor, Dom is the driving force behind the hugely popular Our Time Is Now day party and Wah Wah Live events in South East London, while also regularly appearing as a guest DJ across the globe.

Blending Afrobeat, jazz, house and hip hop with instinct and energy, his sets are vibrant, inclusive and consistently joyful, making him a firm favourite wherever he plays.

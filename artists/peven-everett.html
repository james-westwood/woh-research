Peven Everett

Peven Everett is an artist, multi-instrumentalist, and vocalist born in Harvey, Illinois, just outside Chicago, into a musical family. From an early age, he showed a natural connection to music—first by playing everyday objects as instruments, and soon after receiving his first drum kit from his mother. During school, he studied music under Sherman, John Weber, and David Eanes, quickly standing out as a dedicated and skilled musician.

He held first chair positions across multiple instruments and also spent time student-teaching younger peers. While still young, he performed alongside legendary artists including Dizzy Gillespie and Betty Carter. At seventeen, after earning a perfect score on his exam at the Merit Carl Fischer Music Academy, Peven was awarded a full scholarship to Berklee College of Music. Around this time, he played his first professional performance at Carnegie Hall and soon moved to New York to join Betty Carter.

He served not only as a performer but also as her music director, leading and working with musicians older than himself. While in New York, he collaborated with Wynton Marsalis and appeared in the documentary Marsalis on Music , as well as the accompanying book. He also toured with Buckshot LeFonque, Branford Marsalis’s band. During this period, he was featured in The New York Times Magazine in an article titled “The Next Miles Davis May Be On This Page,” written by Stephen J.

Dubner. After his time in New York, Peven focused on composing and producing his own music, drawing on his ability to play over thirteen instruments. One of his early compositions, “Gabrielle,” created with longtime bandmate Frank Parker Jr., gained recognition and was later featured in the Alvin Ailey production Grace . The track helped bring wider attention to his work, earning coverage from publications including Straight No Chaser , Time Out , and Vibe , and radio play on BBC Radio 1 with Gilles Peterson.

“Gabrielle” is also associated with the emergence of the Two Step genre. Peven has continued to create and release music consistently, with projects including Studio Confession , Latest Craze , Kissing Game , Easy Living , and Power Soul . Alongside these, he has independently released a large body of work, often sharing music directly with his audience to build a close and lasting connection.

His catalog now includes well over 50 albums’ worth of material. In 2006, he received the Heineken Red Star Soul Independent Achiever Award. He has also performed in tribute concerts honoring Prince and Stevie Wonder. Known for his live performances, Peven has appeared on television programs including Late Night with Carson Daly , as well as broadcasts on NBC and WGN. He has been featured in interviews with international DJs and hosts, including Garth Trinidad on KCRW.

In 2008, he was voted Best R&B Artist in Chicago by readers of the Chicago Reader . With a career built on consistent creativity and musicianship, Peven Everett continues to make music and share it with an audience that has grown steadily over time.
